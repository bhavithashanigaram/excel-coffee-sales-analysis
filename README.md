# excel-coffee-sales-analysis
Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel for sales analysis and business insights.



##  Objective
- Analyze coffee shop sales performance.
- Identify peak sales hours.
- Determine the best-selling coffee products.
- Track sales trends by day and month.
- Compare revenue generated by different coffee types.
- Generate actionable business insights using Excel.


##  Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Dataset:** Coffee Shop Sales Dataset
- **Purpose:** Educational and portfolio project.

> **Note:** The dataset used in this project was obtained from Kaggle for learning and demonstration purposes.

##  Key Insights

- Afternoon recorded the highest overall sales.
- Sales peaked during late morning and afternoon hours.
- Latte and Americano with Milk generated the highest revenue.
- March and October showed strong monthly sales performance.
- Espresso contributed the least revenue among the products analyzed.
